Privacy Policy

UniverseIT mobile app and platform · Last updated 16 November 2026

UniverseIT is campus management software operated by SAPTECHS Private Limited. Schools, colleges and trusts subscribe to it. This app release is intended for adults aged 18 and over: parents, staff, and adult students. Available features and records depend on the account and permissions provided by the institution.

Who is responsible for your data

This distinction decides who you ask for what, so it is worth stating plainly.

  • Your institution is the Data Fiduciary. It decides what is recorded about you, who may see it, and how long it is kept. Information comes from institution records and from features you use in the app.
  • SAPTECHS Private Limited is the Data Processor. We store and process that data on your institution's instructions, and we do not decide what is collected or use it for our own purposes.

Requests to see, correct or erase your records therefore go to your institution first. We help them act on such a request; we cannot grant one over their head.

GPS and staff attendance

When staff initiate an attendance punch or a location-based attendance exception, the app requests foreground location permission and obtains the device's current latitude, longitude and accuracy. These are sent to the institution's service to validate and record the attendance event. Authorized staff can also use their current location to configure approved attendance locations.

The app does not continuously track device location or request background location for these attendance features. If permission is denied, location-dependent attendance actions cannot be completed; contact your institution for an alternative procedure. You can change permissions in device settings. Revoking permission does not delete previous attendance records.

Attendance location records are retained with attendance records according to institutional instructions and applicable requirements, and are accessible to authorized personnel according to their roles.

What the app does not collect

  • No microphone or audio recording. Users may choose to attach an existing audio file to a message.
  • No device address-book, call-log or SMS access. Files are accessed when you deliberately select or share them through an app feature.
  • No advertising identifier, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What we never do with it

  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not use your data, or your children's, to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not show one institution another institution's data. Each subscriber's records are held in a separate database container.

Student users and children's records

This release does not permit direct student access for users under 18 or students whose age has not been established from the institution's records. Parents and authorized staff may access children's institutional records according to their roles. Parents or guardians can contact the institution or our privacy contact about access, corrections, account closure or deletion requests concerning a child.

  • The institution is responsible for authorizing access and obtaining required permissions or consent for children's records. Contact the institution or our privacy contact about improper access.
  • A parent account sees only their own children, and staff see only what their role and campus allow. Access to student records is logged.

We do not direct advertising or behavioural profiling at children, because the app carries neither.

Who else sees the data

Only the parties needed to run the service:

  • Map services — attendance maps load OpenStreetMap tiles and Leaflet resources from OpenStreetMap and unpkg. Requests disclose normal connection information such as IP address; tile requests identify the geographic area displayed.
  • SAP — the app runs on SAP Business Technology Platform and SAP HANA Cloud, hosted in India.
  • Notification delivery — Expo, Google (FCM) and Apple (APNs) process push tokens and notification payloads to deliver alerts to your device. These providers may process data outside India; institutional database hosting in India does not mean all delivery processing stays in India. See Expo's privacy policy for its processing practices.
  • Message and payment providers engaged by your institution, where it has switched those on — for example an SMS, email or WhatsApp gateway, or a payment gateway that handles a fee payment. Card and bank details are handled by that payment provider and are not stored by us.

We disclose data otherwise only where the law requires it.

Security

  • Connections to the institution service use HTTPS/TLS. Third-party map and delivery services are described above.
  • Sign-in tokens are held in the device's secure keystore, are short-lived, and are revoked when you sign out.
  • Your password is verified by the identity provider and is never stored by the app.
  • Each institution's data sits in its own isolated database container.

How long it is kept

Your institution sets retention, because these are its records — academic and financial records typically have to be kept for years under education and tax rules. When a subscription ends, its data is returned or deleted on the institution's instruction. Notification tokens are discarded as soon as a device stops being reachable or you sign out.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting, and coordinate institutional record requests with the responsible institution.

Deleting your account

Accounts are provided by your institution. A limited account-closure option may deactivate sign-in when no institution grants access; this does not itself erase institutional records. To request account and data deletion, ask your institution's office, or write to the address above and we will route it to them. Where a record must be retained by law, we will say so and tell you which rule requires it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and we will tell institutions in advance of any change that materially affects their users.
