Sign-in

Auth is real as of 2026-08-23: /mobile-auth signs in against IAS and exchanges for an XSUAA token, then keeps the session on a rotating refresh token bound to this install. The demo-role picker on the phone is the dev-mock path, taken only where the server reports SIMULATED — no IAS binding, or MOBILE_AUTH_LIVE unset. Ask /mobile-auth/authMode(); never assume.
