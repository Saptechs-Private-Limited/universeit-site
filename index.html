01 / 07 Deletion

Can anyone delete
a valid record?

  • No user, no admin, no script — hard delete is refused platform-wide
  • Records are retired instead, stamped with who did it and when
  • Rows removed outside the app trip a daily count check, flagged critical

Who is using which
application?

  • Every login recorded server-side, with activity, IP and device
  • Who opened Finance, when and for how long — a row per app per session
  • Access records are read-only: nobody can write or edit one through the API

03 / 07 Change history

Who modified this,
and when?

  • Every change captured field by field, old value to new value
  • Refused attempts are logged too, with the reason they were refused
  • A tenure can be ended, never extended; a status cannot skip its approver

04 / 07 Personal data

Who looked at that
Aadhaar or bank account?

  • Aadhaar, PAN, bank, mobile and email masked on every read, everywhere
  • They cannot be filtered or searched either — no rebuilding a number
  • Unmasking is audited: who, which field, when, and why they needed it

05 / 07 Approvals

Do different actions
need different approvals?

  • 35 approval points, each set by the institution to approve automatically, go to an approver, or refuse
  • Steps run in sequence — dept head, then finance, then principal
  • A missed deadline escalates to a person; nothing is ever auto-approved

    Hierarchy

    Data isolation is enforced by tenant, and again by campus.

    Every institution gets its own HANA container. Inside it, every read and every write is filtered again by branch scope, driven by the model rather than by handler code, and fail-closed: a user with no resolvable scope sees nothing, not everything. A row marked GLOBAL is inherited read-only by every campus; a campus that needs a variant authors its own.

    ◫

    One container per tenant

    Not a shared table with a tenant column. Each subscribing institution is deployed into its own HDI container, so the isolation is physical, not a WHERE clause someone can forget.

    01 · Admissions

    Admission Process

    Seat quota model: Board / National Entrance Counselling (EAMCET · JEE · NEET) reserves convener-quota seats round by round; Walk-in / Direct Admission fills management, NRI and reverted seats via own test/merit, NRI route or spot / mop-up drive. The waitlist is worked once, after counselling concludes.

    Drawing the flow…

    What the system does about it

    • A seat matrix per programme, split by quota — convener, management, and NRI or Category-C. The quota is carried on the student's own record rather than inferred from how they arrived.Verified
    • Importing a counselling allotment list creates provisional records and locks the allotted seats: they cannot be offered to a walk-in until they lapse or revert. Total admissions cannot exceed the sanctioned intake.Verified
    • Seats not reported against lapse to the next round, and after the final round the unfilled convener seats revert to the institute for the spot or mop-up drive.Verified
    • Fee-reimbursement eligibility and the claim are both recorded, and the fee plan follows the quota — the AFRC convener fee, or management, or NRI.Verified
    • The waitlist is worked once, after counselling concludes and the reverted seats are known — a single downstream step, not a re-check at every round.
    • NRI and Category-C quotas are real and sized in the matrix, with no separate sponsor-document gate. An offer carries a date and a validity, but no offer letter is generated, and there is no university enrolment number beyond the section and roll.
    • Required fields and references are enforced on both the application and the student. Status moves only through the approval flow, never by direct edit. Personal identifiers are masked, and records are retired rather than deleted.

    Employee Lifecycle & Leave

    Drawing the flow…

    What the system does about it

    Fee Collection & Receipt

    Fee structure by quota with regulated-fee provenance; demand by head and term; payment by any mode from the admin-configured registry (each with its own GL account) or govt fee-reimbursement; receipt, auto GL, dues & late-fee cycle; refund per policy.

    Drawing the flow…

    What the system does about it

    • A fee structure per quota and course, with the regulator's own order number stored behind a convener fee — the provenance travels with the amount.Verified
    • The government reimbursement claim is recorded and credited against the student's tuition.Verified
    • Payment modes are an administrator-configurable registry, each carrying its own ledger account. A new mode is added without a code change; there is no fixed list.Verified

    General Ledger & Voucher Posting

    Trust / society books: receipt · payment · journal · contra vouchers, double-entry guard, TDS deduction line on vendor payments, GST input-credit register, statutory payables, BRS, period close. Audit / ITR / FCRA are done outside the system on its exports.

    Drawing the flow…

    What the system does about it

    • A vendor payment deducts TDS as a real line on the voucher, and that line is the source of the quarterly non-salary return.Verified
    • A chart of accounts per trust and branch, kept on society or trust books with 12A and 80G exemption, across four voucher types: receipt, payment, journal and contra.
    • GST runs both ways: a purchase's input tax goes into one register and the GST charged on fees into an output register, with e-invoice IRN once the institution's GSP account is connected. Return filing and portal reconciliation happen outside the system.

    Drawing the flow…

    What the system does about it

    One person, many schools, many hats.
