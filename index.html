Every combination is its own course.

Subjects belong to the course. A different set of subjects is a different course — which is how electives and alternatives are carried.

Pass rules live on the course.

Eligibility and pass conditions are part of the course set-up — per board, per programme, per semester.

Grades follow the board.

Its own marks-to-grade scheme, and its own rules. ICSE's English plus the best four. CBSE's best two of three periodic tests. NEP 2020 holistic progress cards with no marks at all.

Exams carry their own weight.

Each exam type has its own weightage, maximum marks and method of evaluation. Term cards combine them by the weights you set.

No lead goes cold.

You can see the seats.

Fees

Collected on time. Chased without anyone chasing.

One dues register, fed by
  • Tuition
  • Hostel
  • Mess
  • Transport

→ One dues register

Reminders before and after.

Before the due date, and again when it goes overdue — SMS, WhatsApp, email or app notification, in the family's own language.

Late fines, calculated.

Worked out automatically, on your rule. Nobody adds them by hand.

A defaulters list that acts.

Ranked by amount outstanding. Remind every defaulter in one click, or one family at a time.

Consequences you control.

Hall tickets held back while fees are outstanding, set per course by you. No-dues certificates issued from the same register, and revocable.

Fee structures by class or course, split into instalments, with concessions through approval. Refunds and concession reversals go through approval and post straight to the ledger. Parents see their dues and receipts on the app.

How it is built

If it should not happen, it should not be possible.

Nothing slips through

Rules are enforced, not remembered.

A user cannot save what your institution does not allow. The check runs on the screen, and again on the server.

Approvals are gates, not formalities.

Thirty-nine approval points. The right person says yes before the thing happens — not after it has to be undone.

Set once, applied to everyone.

Mandatory fields, limits and eligibility are set by you and hold for every user, every day, without reminding.

Nobody waits

Dhi, your in-app assistant.

Manuals, documents and PDFs are provided. Dhi also sits inside the app, guiding each user to the right app and the right task.

Report it where it happened.

Raise a ticket from the same screen, without leaving the work or finding out who to email.

No one is stuck until somebody replies.

A small doubt is answered in the app. Work carries on while the ticket is being looked at.

A developer should never have to ask a user why they did that. If the software allowed it, the software is the one that got it wrong.

AI, on your terms

Off by default. Yours to turn on.

You never need AI to use UniverseIT. Nothing you enter, approve or report depends on a model. Dhi guides by rules, not by guessing. The product works exactly the same with AI switched off.

Migration and switching over

The part you are afraid of.

Nobody is afraid of a feature list. They are afraid of a June where nothing works.

The path
  1. Your spreadsheets
  2. Every row checked
  3. Test copy
  4. Rehearse
  5. Go live

Bring what you already have.

Your existing registers and spreadsheets are the starting point. No one re-types a year of records.

Nothing saves until it is clean.

Every row is checked before anything is written. Errors come back to you as a list, not as a surprise in June.

Rehearse in a test copy.

Load it, look at it, run a fee collection and a payroll against it. Change your mind. Load it again.

Go live when you are ready.

Not when a contract says so. The old system stays until you no longer need it.
